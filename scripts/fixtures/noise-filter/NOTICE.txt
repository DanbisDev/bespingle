Recordings used by scripts/noise-filter-smoke.mjs to measure the noise filter.
Each is a short excerpt, mixed to mono and re-encoded as 48 kHz Opus. All three
source recordings are in the public domain.

voice.webm: seconds 9-17 of "Sonnet-by Christina Rossetti.ogg", read by Shanel.
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sonnet-by_Christina_Rossetti.ogg

keyboard.webm: seconds 10-15 of "Computer keyboard.ogg", recorded by russiandoll.
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Computer_keyboard.ogg

kitchen.webm: seconds 28-33 of "Kitchen tidy up.ogg", recorded by starlite.
https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Kitchen_tidy_up.ogg
